About Us

We repair air conditioners and household appliances in Riyadh, with a simple promise: inspect first, explain clearly and repair only after you approve.

What we do

We repair, install and maintain air conditioners, and repair washing machines, dryers, refrigerators, freezers, dishwashers, electric water heaters, ovens, stoves, microwaves and kitchen hoods.

Who we serve

Homeowners, tenants and landlords, as well as offices, shops, restaurants and other small businesses in Riyadh.

  • Apartments and villas
  • Offices and shops
  • Restaurants and small businesses
  • Property managers and landlords

How we approach repairs

A good repair starts with finding the real cause. We inspect the appliance, explain what we find, and discuss the options: clean, repair or replace a part. We do not recommend replacement parts before diagnosis.

Clear estimates

Repair costs depend on the appliance, the fault, the parts and the labor. We explain the estimate before starting, and work begins only after your approval.

Where we work

We serve Riyadh. Share your neighborhood when you contact us and we will confirm that we can reach you.
