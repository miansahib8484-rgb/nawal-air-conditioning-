AC Installation

A good installation affects cooling, running costs and how long the unit lasts. We install new and replacement air conditioners, and the right method depends on your equipment and your property.

From our jobs

  • Fitting a new indoor split unit
  • Working on a wall-mounted split AC
  • Brazing a copper refrigerant pipe
  • Split AC with its outdoor unit

What installation includes

  • New split AC installation
  • Replacing an old unit
  • Cassette and ducted AC installation
  • Central AC installation
  • Choosing the position of indoor and outdoor units
  • Wall brackets and mounting
  • Refrigerant piping and insulation
  • Condensate drain routing
  • Electrical connection
  • Testing and commissioning

What affects the result

  • The size of the unit compared with the room it serves.
  • Wall strength and the position of the mounting bracket.
  • The distance between the indoor and outdoor units.
  • Shade and airflow around the outdoor unit.
  • The electrical supply and breaker available.
  • A drain route with enough slope to a safe outlet.

How we diagnose and repair

  1. 1

    We confirm the room, wall, power supply and unit details with you.

  2. 2

    We agree where the indoor and outdoor units will go and what materials are needed.

  3. 3

    We mount the units, connect piping, drain and power.

  4. 4

    We test the system, check cooling and show you how to use the controls.

Repair or replace?

We can discuss installing a unit you have already bought or help you think through capacity for a room. Replacement jobs can include removing the old unit where agreed. Please confirm the scope and the estimate before work starts.

Safety first

Installation involves working at height, electrical supply and refrigerant piping. Leave it to trained technicians. A poorly installed unit can leak, trip breakers or damage the compressor.

Care tips

  • Check that the unit's capacity suits the room before you buy.
  • Keep the manual and any warranty documents safe.
  • Ask where the outdoor unit and drain will be positioned before the day.
  • Have the first service done when it is due.
