Central AC Repair & Maintenance in Riyadh

Central and ducted air conditioning cools a whole villa, office or shop through ducts and vents, so a fault can affect many rooms. We inspect central AC systems, find the fault and explain what it needs before any repair starts. Tell us about your property and the system and we will confirm what we can do.

Common symptoms

  • Some rooms cool and others do not
  • Weak airflow from the vents
  • Whole system not cooling
  • Water leaking from the unit or ceiling
  • Noisy fan, blower or compressor
  • System turning on and off repeatedly
  • Tripping the breaker
  • Thermostat not responding

Possible causes

  • Dirty filters, coils or blocked ducts.
  • Blower or fan motor faults.
  • Low refrigerant caused by a leak.
  • Blocked drain tray or drain line.
  • Thermostat or control faults.
  • Dampers or airflow balance problems.
  • Compressor, capacitor or contactor faults.

How we diagnose and repair

  1. 1

    We ask about the system type, the property and the fault.

  2. 2

    We check the thermostat, filters, airflow and drain.

  3. 3

    We inspect the blower, outdoor or package unit, capacitor and refrigerant.

  4. 4

    We explain what we found, give an estimate and repair after your approval.

Repair or replace?

Regular central AC maintenance catches problems early, which matters for large systems. For bigger repairs we explain the options and the cost before work starts.

Safety first

Switch the system off at the breaker if you smell burning, see sparks or find water near electrical parts. Do not open the unit or touch wiring yourself.

Care tips

  • Replace or clean filters on time.
  • Keep vents and return grilles clear.
  • Have the system serviced before the hot season.
  • Tell us about any uneven cooling early.

Questions about this service

Do you repair ducted and package AC units?

Tell us about your property and the system on WhatsApp and we will confirm what we can do before you book.

Why are some rooms not cooling?

It can be airflow balance, a blocked duct, dirty filters or a faulty damper. An inspection confirms the cause.

Do you offer central AC maintenance?

Yes. Regular maintenance includes cleaning, airflow, electrical and drain checks.

Do you maintain central and ducted AC for villas and offices in Riyadh?

Yes. Tell us the system type (ducted, package or central), the tonnage and the building and we will confirm what the visit covers.

Why is only one room not cooling on my central AC?

It can be a closed or blocked duct, a damper problem, an airflow imbalance or a fault in the air handler. An inspection finds the cause.
