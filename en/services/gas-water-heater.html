Gas Water Heater & Heater Repair

Gas water heaters (geysers) and gas room heaters depend on a safe flame, a clean burner and working safety devices. We inspect gas units that will not light, go out, give weak heat or smell wrong. Because gas is involved, safety comes first, and we confirm the unit type and brand with you before booking.

From our jobs

  • Heating element bank taken out for inspection

Common symptoms

  • Pilot light or burner will not light
  • Flame goes out after a few seconds
  • Weak flame or yellow flame
  • Not enough hot water from a gas geyser
  • Clicking ignition but no flame
  • Smell of gas near the unit
  • Soot marks or a smell of burning
  • Heater turns itself off

Possible causes

  • Blocked burner or dirty gas jets.
  • Faulty ignition, thermocouple or flame sensor.
  • Low gas supply, regulator or cylinder connection problems.
  • Worn gas hose or loose fittings.
  • Dust and soot build-up inside the unit.
  • Water flow or pressure problems on gas geysers.
  • Poor ventilation affecting the flame.

How we diagnose and repair

  1. 1

    You tell us the unit type, brand and the fault, and whether you smell gas.

  2. 2

    We check the gas connection, hose and regulator.

  3. 3

    We inspect the burner, ignition, flame sensor and safety devices.

  4. 4

    We explain what we found and give an estimate before any repair begins.

Repair or replace?

Cleaning, a new ignition part, a sensor or a hose often solves the problem. If a unit is very old or unsafe to repair we will say so honestly so you can decide on a replacement.

Safety first

If you smell gas, do not switch any light or appliance on or off. Close the gas valve, open windows, leave the area and contact your gas supplier or emergency services. Do not try to repair gas parts yourself, and never use a unit with a damaged hose.

Care tips

  • Check the gas hose and connections for damage before each winter.
  • Use gas units only in well-ventilated spaces and follow the manual.
  • Do not cover or block vents and flues.
  • Stop using a gas heater that keeps going out and have it inspected.

Questions about this service

Which gas units do you look at?

Gas water heaters (geysers) and gas room heaters. Send the brand and model on WhatsApp and we will confirm before you book.

My gas geyser keeps going out. Why?

A blocked burner, faulty thermocouple or low gas supply can all cause it. Stop using it and have it inspected.

Is a yellow flame dangerous?

A yellow or weak flame can mean a dirty burner or poor combustion. Stop using the unit and have it checked.

Do you repair electric water heaters too?

Yes. See our water heater repair page.

Why does my gas geyser keep going out?

A blocked burner, a faulty thermocouple or flame sensor, or low gas supply can all cause it. Stop using it and have it inspected, and follow the gas safety steps above if you smell gas.
