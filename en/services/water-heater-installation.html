Water Heater Installation

We install and replace water heaters (geysers) in homes, apartments and shops in Riyadh. Tell us the type of heater, its size and where it will be fitted, and we will confirm what the installation involves before any work begins.

When you need it

  • Your old water heater is leaking or badly corroded
  • You bought a new geyser and need it fitted
  • You are moving the heater to a new position
  • The old unit keeps failing and repairs no longer make sense
  • A new bathroom or kitchen needs hot water

What the installation involves

  • Choosing a safe, dry position with room for service access.
  • Mounting the unit securely on the wall or floor.
  • Connecting water inlet and outlet pipes and fitting a safety valve where the unit needs one.
  • Connecting the power supply safely.
  • Filling the tank, testing for leaks, and checking the temperature and controls.

How we diagnose and repair

  1. 1

    You send the unit details, a photo of the location and what you need.

  2. 2

    We confirm what the installation involves and give an estimate.

  3. 3

    After your approval we remove the old unit if needed and install the new one.

  4. 4

    We test heating, leaks and controls and show you how to use it.

Repair or replace?

If an old heater only needs a new element, thermostat or valve, we will tell you honestly so you do not replace it unnecessarily.

Safety first

Do not install a water heater yourself. Water, electricity and pressure together are dangerous. If the old unit is leaking, switch off the power at the breaker and close the water inlet until we arrive.

Care tips

  • Choose a size that matches how many people use hot water.
  • Leave space around the unit for inspection and servicing.
  • Keep the manual and the safety valve instructions.
  • Ask for the old unit to be checked before deciding to replace it.

Questions about this service

Do you supply the water heater?

Please ask us on WhatsApp. If you already bought a unit, send us its details and we will confirm the installation.

Can you replace my old heater in the same place?

Usually yes. Send a photo of the current heater and its connections and we will confirm.

Will you tell me the cost first?

Yes. We confirm what is needed and give an estimate before any work starts.

How much does geyser installation cost in Riyadh?

It depends on the unit, the location and the plumbing and electrical work. Send details of the heater and where it will go and we will confirm what the job involves and give an estimate.
