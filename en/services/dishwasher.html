Dishwasher Repair

Dishwashers fail in simple ways, like blocked filters, and in complex ways, like pump or board faults. We inspect the machine, explain the cause and repair it only after you approve.

Common symptoms

  • Will not start
  • Not filling with water
  • Not draining or standing water inside
  • Leaking
  • Dishes come out dirty
  • Dishes not drying
  • Unusual noises
  • Door will not latch
  • An error code on the display

Possible causes

  • A clogged filter or drain hose.
  • A failed drain pump or inlet valve.
  • Blocked or damaged spray arms.
  • A faulty heating element or temperature sensor.
  • A worn door latch or door seal.
  • A detergent dispenser or control board fault.

How we diagnose and repair

  1. 1

    You describe the symptom and any error code.

  2. 2

    We check the filter, drain, spray arms, door and seals.

  3. 3

    We test the pump, valve, heater and sensors.

  4. 4

    We explain the fault, give an estimate and repair after your approval.

Repair or replace?

Cleaning the filter and drain solves many problems. Pumps, valves, heating elements, latches and seals can be replaced. For an older machine with a failed control board, we compare repair cost with replacement.

Safety first

Turn off power and water before checking anything. Do not use the dishwasher if water is near sockets or if it leaks continuously.

Care tips

  • Scrape food off plates before loading.
  • Clean the filter regularly.
  • Use dishwasher detergent and rinse aid as the manual advises.
  • Run a cleaning cycle with a dishwasher cleaner from time to time.

Questions about this service

My dishwasher leaves dishes dirty. What is wrong?

A dirty filter, blocked spray arms, too much loading or a heating problem can all be the cause. We check these on inspection.

How should I describe the problem before booking?

Tell us the brand, what happens, when it started and any error code. A photo or short video on WhatsApp helps.

There is water left at the bottom. Is that normal?

A small amount in the sump can be normal. Standing water after a cycle usually means a drain problem.
