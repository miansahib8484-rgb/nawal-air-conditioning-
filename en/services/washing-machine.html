Washing Machine Repair

We repair front-load, top-load and fully automatic washing machines, as well as washer-dryer units. We diagnose first, so you know whether a part needs cleaning, repair or replacement before you approve any work.

Common symptoms

  • Will not turn on or start a cycle
  • Not filling with water
  • Not draining or water left in the drum
  • Not spinning or stopping mid-cycle
  • Leaking water
  • Loud noise or heavy vibration
  • Door will not lock or will not open
  • Error code on the display
  • Clothes not clean or a musty smell
  • Water not heating

Possible causes

  • A blocked drain pump, filter or hose.
  • A faulty water inlet valve.
  • A worn drive belt, motor brushes or drum bearings.
  • A damaged door lock or door seal.
  • A failed pressure sensor, heating element or control board.
  • An unbalanced load or worn suspension.

How we diagnose and repair

  1. 1

    We check the symptoms, any error code and the installation: hoses, levelling and load.

  2. 2

    We inspect the pump, hoses, door lock, belt and motor.

  3. 3

    We test the sensors, heater and control board where needed.

  4. 4

    We explain the fault and give an estimate, repair after your approval, then run a test cycle.

Repair or replace?

Clearing a blocked filter or hose is often enough. Worn belts, pumps, valves and bearings can be replaced. For an older machine with major drum or board damage, we explain the cost of repair compared with replacement.

Safety first

Unplug the machine and turn off the water before checking anything. If there is water near the plug or the breaker trips, stop using the machine.

Care tips

  • Avoid overloading the drum.
  • Leave the door ajar after washing to let it dry.
  • Clean the detergent drawer and drain filter from time to time.
  • Use the amount of detergent the manufacturer recommends.

Questions about this service

My washing machine will not drain. What can I check?

After unplugging it, check the drain filter and hose for blockages if your manual shows how. If water remains or you are unsure, contact us.

Do you repair both front-load and top-load machines?

Yes, we repair front-load, top-load and fully automatic machines. Tell us the brand and model and we will confirm.

The machine shakes violently. Is that serious?

Heavy vibration can damage the machine and the floor. Stop it, check the load and levelling, and if it continues we inspect the bearings and suspension.
