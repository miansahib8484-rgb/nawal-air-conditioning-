Pool Pump & Filter Repair

The pool pump keeps the water moving through the filter and the heater. When it stops, makes noise or leaks, the whole pool system suffers. We inspect pool pumps and related equipment, find the fault and explain what it needs before any repair starts.

Common symptoms

  • Pump will not start or hums but does not turn
  • Pump not pushing water or losing prime
  • Loud grinding or screeching noise
  • Water leaking from the pump or seals
  • Pump trips the breaker
  • Weak flow or low filter pressure
  • Air bubbles returning to the pool
  • Pump gets very hot

Possible causes

  • Worn motor bearings or a failed motor capacitor.
  • Blocked basket, impeller or strainer.
  • Air leak on the suction side or a worn lid seal.
  • Failed shaft seal causing leaks.
  • Dirty filter restricting the flow.
  • Wiring, timer or power supply faults.
  • Running the pump dry.

How we diagnose and repair

  1. 1

    We ask about the pump brand, the fault and when it started.

  2. 2

    We check the power supply, timer and wiring.

  3. 3

    We inspect the motor, capacitor, impeller, seals and baskets.

  4. 4

    We check the filter and the water flow.

  5. 5

    We explain the fault, give an estimate and repair after your approval.

Repair or replace?

Seals, capacitors, bearings and impellers can often be replaced. If the motor is badly worn we explain the cost compared with a new pump.

Safety first

Switch the pump off at the breaker before touching it, and never work on pool electrics while wet or standing in water. If the pump trips the power or you see water near electrical parts, keep people away and contact us.

Care tips

  • Empty the pump basket regularly.
  • Never run the pump without water.
  • Check the lid seal and fittings for air leaks.
  • Clean or backwash the filter according to the manual.

Questions about this service

Why does my pool pump lose prime?

Usually an air leak on the suction side, a worn lid seal or a blocked basket or line. An inspection confirms the cause.

My pool pump is noisy. Can it be fixed?

Worn bearings, a blocked impeller or cavitation from low water flow can cause it. Stop using it and have it inspected.

Do you repair pool heaters too?

Yes. See our pool heater and heat pump page.

Will you tell me the cost first?

Yes. We inspect first, explain the fault and give an estimate before any repair starts.

Why is my pool pump not working or losing prime?

A tripped breaker or failed capacitor, a blocked basket or impeller, or an air leak on the suction side are common causes. We inspect to find which.
