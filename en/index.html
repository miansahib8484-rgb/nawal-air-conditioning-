Before the heat

A simple AC checklist for Riyadh's summer

A few checks before the hot months can save you a breakdown. Book a service if anything on the list looks wrong.

  • Clean or replace the filters, following your unit's manual.
  • Switch the AC on early in the season and check it cools properly.
  • Make sure the outdoor unit is clear of dust, boxes and plants.
  • Look for water around the indoor unit or a blocked drain.
  • Listen for new rattling, buzzing or squealing noises.
  • Notice any musty smell or weak airflow, and have the unit serviced.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does a repair cost?

    It depends on the appliance, the fault, the parts needed and the labor. We do not quote a fixed price over the phone for unknown faults. After inspection you receive a clear estimate before any work begins.

    Do you inspect before repairing?

    Yes. We diagnose the problem first, then explain whether the part needs cleaning, repair or replacement.

    My AC is low on gas. Can you just refill it?

    Refrigerant is not used up like fuel. A low level usually points to a leak or another fault, so the system should be diagnosed first rather than just topped up.

    Do you replace parts?

    When a part cannot be repaired or is no longer safe to keep, we explain the options and the cost, and replace it only with your approval.

    How do I book an appointment?

    Call us, message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form. Tell us the appliance, the problem and your neighborhood, and we will agree a time.
